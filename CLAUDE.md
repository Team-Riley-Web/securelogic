# securelogic

## Brand names

The product brand is written **`Genesis360`** — one word, no space, no hyphen.
Never `Genesis 360`. This holds everywhere: page titles, Open Graph, nav labels,
headings, body copy, image `alt` text, blog bodies, FAQ answers, and comments.
The trademark form is `Genesis360™`.

Check it on every change that touches copy:

```
npm run check:brand
```

`Secure Logic` (two words) is the parent company and stays as-is wherever the
legal entity is speaking — corporate identity statements, officer titles,
press-release boilerplate and attributions, the footer copyright, IP/ownership
lines. Genesis360 leads everywhere consumer-facing.

Do not rewrite these, they are live identifiers, not brand copy:
`securelogicusa.com`, `info@`/`marty@` addresses, `securelogic.netlify.app`,
social handles (`facebook.com/SecureLogicUSA`, `instagram/securelogicusa`,
`youtube @SecureLogic-Genesis360`), the blog slug
`secure-logic-newfields-ag-botanical-disinfection-partnership`, and
`/docs/Secure-Logic-*.pdf`. `securelogicusa.com` links flip to genesis360.com
only as part of the DNS cutover tracked in TASKS.md.
