Blind Spot #3: The Air Is Infected (Yes, Really)

You can’t see it. You can’t always smell it. But it’s there, floating, settling, reinfecting.

In an enclosed wrestling room, every drop of sweat that hits the mat releases microscopic particles into the air. Skin cells become airborne. Pathogens aerosolize and circulate through your facility’s ventilation system (or lack thereof). Then they settle back down onto your freshly cleaned mats, your athletes’ gear, and their exposed skin.

This is why you can clean everything and still have outbreaks.
