Now more than ever, the health and productivity of poultry farms hinges on effective disinfection practices. Pathogens such as HPAI (Highly Pathogenic Avian Influenza or Bird Flu) can devastate flocks and be detrimental to human health. Rigorous cleaning and disinfection protocols are essential to mitigate these threats. Thankfully, a new disinfectant and way to apply it now exists.

Why Disinfection Matters

Disinfection involves using physical or chemical processes to reduce, remove, inactivate, or destroy pathogenic microorganisms. Regular disinfection of housing, equipment, and water sources in poultry farming is vital to prevent disease outbreaks. The USDA emphasizes that cleaning and disinfection (C&D) are crucial in controlling the spread of microorganisms between animals, locations, or people (USDA).

Challenges with Traditional Disinfection Methods

Many poultry farms rely on traditional chemical disinfectants, which come with several challenges:

  • Harsh Chemical Residues: Toxic residues are left behind by some disinfectants. These residues are not ideal and can harm poultry and workers, requiring additional rinsing and ventilation.
  • Pathogen Resistance: Over time, pathogens can develop resistance to commonly used disinfectants, reducing their effectiveness and necessitating the use of stronger, potentially more hazardous chemicals.
  • Labor and Downtime: Halting operations for extended periods is a known byproduct of many disinfection protocols. Lost productivity and increased labor costs are the result.
  • Environmental Concerns: Runoff from chemical disinfectants can contaminate local water sources, posing risks to surrounding ecosystems.

Protecting Workers and Livestock

Workers and livestock need protection. Adequate disinfection safeguards both. Pathogens can transfer from birds to humans, leading to potential health issues. The USDA’s Defend the Flock program offers resources to help those handling poultry follow proper biosecurity practices, reducing the risk of disease transmission (USDA Defend the Flock).
